# Project Plan

## Full Port Roadmap

The goal is a playable Unreal port of Amulets & Armor from a new game through the original campaign, with working progression, saves, and level transitions. The port will restore multiplayer.

| Milestone | Completion check | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Native level foundation | Import original geometry, materials, collision, moving sectors, and lighting into saved Unreal levels. | Proven on L30 and L151 at GitHub checkpoint `727f482`. |

| 3. Core game systems | Verify combat, enemy AI, items and inventory, equipment, spells, and character progression against the original. | Verified a Guardian fight, Rejuvenate, priest progression, item use, melee equipment, armor mitigation, and dropped-item persistence. |
| 4. Authored levels and events | Import the remaining campaign maps and verify switches, lifts, traps, scripted events, and level exits. Track unsupported features and missing assets by map. | All30 maps have native geometry; all1,942 authored inventory placements are bound. | 5. Presentation and persistence | Restore sky and lighting behavior, audio, menus, HUD, and save/load.
